KEY FEATURES

Everything a modern hostel needs

Twelve tightly-integrated modules that replace registers, notice boards and spreadsheets with one smart, digital platform.

Smart Rooms

Real-time room occupancy, bed capacity enforcement (Single/Double/Triple), floor indexing & one-click allocation.

Leave Management

Digital leave requests for home visits, emergency parent verification, and instant admin approvals with review notes.

South Indian Mess

Authentic weekly breakfast, lunch & dinner menus with timing highlights, 15-day (₹1,800) and 30-day (₹3,000) subscriptions.

Fees & Payments

Transparent digital fee ledgers, payment logging, categorized statements and automated pending/overdue tracking.

Complaints & Support

Categorized grievance submission with live progress tracking (Pending, In Progress, Resolved) and warden resolution notes.

Student Privacy Shield

Confidential profile protection guarding student phone numbers and personal data while offering student self-service editing.
